# decision-quality

**Question:** Were the major orchestration decisions reasonable GIVEN WHAT WAS KNOWN AT THE TIME they were made?

Read each major act with its contemporaneous state, stated why/expecting, and the alternatives then available. Explicitly separate process from luck.

- **5** — Each major move was the defensible best of its live alternatives; stakes-proportionate; reversible where uncertainty was high; stated expectations later tracked reality or triggered appropriate updates.
- **3** — Mostly sound with one move that was hard to justify even contemporaneously.
- **1** — Choices repeatedly indefensible on the information available — regardless of how the outcome landed.
