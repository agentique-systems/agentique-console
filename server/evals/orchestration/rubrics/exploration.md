# exploration

**Question:** Was exploration sized to the decisions it had to inform — neither reflexive agent-spawning nor premature convergence?

- **5** — Explored until the next decision was defensible, then stopped; used prototypes/experiments where analysis would have been slower or weaker; named what each exploration was FOR.
- **3** — Adequate exploration with some waste or one missed cheap probe.
- **1** — Committed on unexamined assumptions, or explored endlessly with no decision consuming the findings.
