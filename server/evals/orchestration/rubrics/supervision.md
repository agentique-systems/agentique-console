# supervision

**Question:** Was running work actually supervised — diagnosed from live data, intervened on when signals warranted?

- **5** — Alarms/silence/contradictions prompted inspection of real state (not stale narration); interventions were proportionate, timely, and explained; no babysitting of healthy sessions.
- **3** — Supervision present but late or coarse once.
- **1** — A visible failure was waited out, or healthy work was disrupted by reflexive poking.
