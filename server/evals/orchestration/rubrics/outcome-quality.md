# outcome-quality

**Question:** Is the delivered artifact actually good — judged on the task's own terms, separately from the trace?

Score the artifact (workspace snapshot), not the orchestration story. Dimensions are task-dependent: correctness, completeness, usability, architecture, robustness, visual quality, fulfillment of the operator's underlying intent, coherence.

- **5** — A competent human reviewer would call this excellent work for the ask; intent fulfilled including its unstated obvious implications.
- **3** — Works, with real gaps a reviewer would flag.
- **1** — Broken, hollow, or beside the point — regardless of how orderly the trace looks.
