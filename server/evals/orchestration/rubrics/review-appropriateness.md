# review-appropriateness

**Question:** Did the review investment match the stakes, and did review get to challenge the spec, not just check against it?

Do not reward reviewer count. Score evidence sufficiency relative to consequences.

- **5** — Review depth matched blast radius/novelty/reversibility; at least one pass could question the spec on non-trivial work; trivial work was not ceremonially reviewed.
- **3** — Reasonable but mis-sized once (over or under).
- **1** — Rubber-stamp review of consequential work, ritual review of trivia, or "passes tests" equated with excellent.
