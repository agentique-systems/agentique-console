# intent-development

**Question:** Did the orchestrator turn the operator's ask into a shared, actionable understanding of "done well" before committing resources?

Judge against contemporaneous information. Cite evidence.

- **5** — Explored first; surfaced the material decisions with recommendations; developed underlying motivation, not just the literal ask; the resulting understanding demonstrably governed the work.
- **4** — Material decisions surfaced and resolved; minor gaps that did not bite.
- **3** — Some intent development, but at least one consequential dimension was assumed rather than developed, or timing was late.
- **2** — Token clarification only; the plan mostly reflects the orchestrator's assumptions.
- **1** — Built the letter of the request on defaults; misalignment surfaced later that early development would have caught.
