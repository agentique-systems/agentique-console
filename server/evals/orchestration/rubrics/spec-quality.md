# spec-quality

**Question:** Was the governing specification (spec artifact, or its de-facto equivalent in briefings) complete where it mattered, honest about open uncertainty, and actually used?

- **5** — Goals, constraints, acceptance criteria a reviewer can check, standing uncertainties/assumptions recorded; briefings and reviews cite it; amendments tracked reality.
- **3** — A real spec exists but misses a consequential dimension, or drifted from reality without amendment.
- **1** — No governing spec beyond the task card; agents worked from divergent understandings.
