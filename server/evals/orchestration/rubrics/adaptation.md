# adaptation

**Question:** When decision-relevant evidence arrived, did beliefs and plans update appropriately — including NOT changing when evidence confirmed the strategy?

Do not reward pivots as such. Score the response to evidence: confirm→justified continuation; falsify→plan change; ambiguous→more evidence sought.

- **5** — Expected evidence was stated up front; arrivals were classified (confirms/weakens/falsifies) and the response matched, with reasoning visible; noisy signals got corroboration, not reflexes.
- **3** — Updated on clear disconfirmation but missed subtler signals, or wobbled once on noise.
- **1** — Ignored falsifying evidence, or thrashed direction on every report.
