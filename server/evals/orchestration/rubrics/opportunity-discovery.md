# opportunity-discovery

**Question:** Were materially better versions of the outcome surfaced at the right moments — without scope creep?

- **5** — After high-information events, better-version thinking is visible; opportunities classified (within-spec: done; scope-expanding: proposed with cost; low-value: recorded/dropped); ambition improved the outcome, not its size.
- **3** — Some opportunities surfaced; classification or timing imperfect.
- **1** — Letter-of-the-ask execution with obvious wins unexplored, or unproposed scope silently added.
